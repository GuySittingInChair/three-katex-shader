# Glossary

One line per word, in alphabetical order, with a link to the page that explains it. Pages marked *(planned)*
don't exist yet; until then the link goes to the closest page that does.

- **A* search**: a shortest-route search that ranks places by steps taken plus a guess of the steps left; with
  a guess that never overestimates, it is guaranteed to find a shortest route. → [Pathfinding Race: in plain
  English](sketches/pathfindingRace.md)
- **absolute magnitude**: how bright a star would look from a standard 10 parsecs away, so stars can be
  compared fairly. → [Parallax: measuring the distance to the stars](parallax-and-distance.md)
- **actions**: extra buttons a sketch adds to the Params panel, like "Poke the dish". → [The sketch format:
  every field](sketch-format.md)
- **adaptive landscape**: a map of possible animals where height is how well each would do; evolution climbs it.
  Also called a fitness landscape. → [Adaptive landscapes](adaptive-landscape.md)
- **additive blending**: overlapping light adds up and gets brighter, like overlapping torch beams. → [Colour
  2: blending, adding, dimming and gain](colour-blending-and-gain.md)
- **agent-based model**: a simulation of many simple individuals (agents), each following its own rules, to
  see what the crowd does. → [Slime Mould Rail Planner: in plain English](sketches/physarumRail.md)
- **aggregation**: things clumping together and sticking. → [Diffusion-Limited Aggregation: in plain
  English](sketches/diffusionLimitedAggregation.md)
- **alpha**: a colour's fourth number, how solid it is: 1 solid, 0 invisible. → [Colour 1: RGB, three
  numbers](rgb.md)
- **arcsecond**: a 3,600th of a degree; a milliarcsecond (mas) is a thousandth of that. → [Parallax: measuring
  the distance to the stars](parallax-and-distance.md)
- **array**: a numbered list of values, written `[ ]`, counting from 0. → [Reading JavaScript 4: lists
  (arrays) and labelled boxes (objects)](js-arrays-and-objects.md)
- **async / await**: marks a recipe that can pause to wait for a download without freezing the page. →
  [Reading JavaScript 5: loading data (`fetch`, `Promise`, `await`)](js-promises-and-fetch.md)
- **attribute**: a list holding one value per corner of a shape (position, colour). → [Drawing in 3D 2: mesh,
  geometry and material](mesh-geometry-material.md)
- **bell curve**: the hill shape of heights, exam marks and errors: most near the middle, few at the edges. →
  [Galton Board: in plain English](sketches/galtonBoard.md)
- **bisection**: finding where something crosses zero by repeatedly halving a range and keeping the half where
  the sign changes. → [Lagrange Point Parking: in plain English](sketches/lagrangeParking.md)
- **bit**: one binary digit, 0 or 1: the smallest piece of information. → [Maxwell's Demon: in plain
  English](sketches/maxwellsDemon.md)
- **black hole shadow**: the dark disc where light aimed too close to a black hole falls in, about 5.2 of its
  mass units across. → [Black Hole Lensing: in plain English](sketches/blackHoleLensing.md)
- **black ring**: a black hole shaped like a ring (a doughnut), possible only with an extra dimension of
  space; its spin keeps it from collapsing. → [Dark Saturn: in plain English](sketches/darkSaturn.md)
- **black Saturn**: an exact solution of Einstein's equations in five-dimensional spacetime: a round black
  hole inside a black ring, in balance (Elvang and Figueras, 2007). → [Dark Saturn: in plain
  English](sketches/darkSaturn.md)
- **boids**: Craig Reynolds's simulated birds (1987): separation, alignment and cohesion with nearby
  neighbours make a flock. → [Hawks and Boids: in plain English](sketches/hawksAndBoids.md)
- **Born rule**: in quantum mechanics, the chance of finding a particle somewhere is the square of its wave's
  size there. → [Double Slit, With Eraser: in plain English](sketches/doubleSlitEraser.md)
- **breadth-first search**: exploring a network in rings: everything one step away, then two steps, and so on;
  finds the fewest steps to everywhere. → [Six Degrees of Poop: in plain English](sketches/smallWorld.md)
- **buoyancy**: the upward push on something lighter than what surrounds it, such as warm air in cool air. →
  [Schlieren: Seeing Hot Air: in plain English](sketches/schlierenPlume.md)
- **butterfly diagram**: a chart of where on the Sun the sunspots were, year by year; each 11-year cycle makes
  a pair of wing shapes as spots start far from the equator and drift towards it. → [The Sun's Eleven-Year
  Heartbeat: in plain English](sketches/sunCycle.md)
- **Bézier curve**: a smooth curve pulled towards a few control points, as in drawing programs. → [De
  Casteljau Bézier: in plain English](sketches/bezierDeCasteljau.md)
- **bifurcation**: the moment one steady behaviour splits into two, like a population starting to boom and
  bust. → [Bifurcation Diagram: in plain English](sketches/bifurcationDiagram.md)
- **binary file (`.bin`)**: numbers packed as raw bytes to keep downloads small. → [Reading JavaScript 5:
  loading data (`fetch`, `Promise`, `await`)](js-promises-and-fetch.md)
- **binomial distribution**: how often you get each number of heads from a set of coin flips. → [Galton Board:
  in plain English](sketches/galtonBoard.md)
- **bloom**: another name for glow. → [Glow: making bright things shine](glow.md)
- **burn-in**: running a rule many times and throwing the results away, so it settles first. → [Bifurcation
  Diagram: in plain English](sketches/bifurcationDiagram.md)
- **camera**: the viewpoint the 3D scene is photographed from. → [Drawing in 3D 1: the scene and the
  camera](scene-and-camera.md)
- **CanvasTexture**: a picture drawn on a hidden flat canvas, then used in the scene. → [Textures: pictures a
  shader can look things up in](textures.md)
- **Carrington rotation**: one turn of the Sun as seen from the Earth, 27.3 days, numbered since 1853; also
  the Sun's own map grid (Carrington longitude). → [The Sun's Eleven-Year Heartbeat: in plain
  English](sketches/sunCycle.md)
- **cascade**: a chain of failures, each one causing the next. → [Cascading Blackout: in plain
  English](sketches/cascadingBlackout.md)
- **category**: the gallery shelf a sketch sits on. → [The sketch format: every field](sketch-format.md)
- **catenoid**: the hourglass-shaped soap film stretched between two rings held apart. → [Helicoid ↔ Catenoid:
  in plain English](sketches/helicoidCatenoid.md)
- **caustic**: bright lines of light focused by a curved surface, like sunlight on a pool floor. → [Caustics:
  in plain English](sketches/causticsLight.md)
- **cellular automaton**: a grid of squares that each update by a simple rule about their neighbours. →
  [Conway's Game of Life: in plain English](sketches/gameOfLife.md)
- **centrifugal effect**: in a turning frame, the apparent outward push felt by everything, like on a
  roundabout. → [Lagrange Point Parking: in plain English](sketches/lagrangeParking.md)
- **Chandra**: NASA's X-ray space telescope, in orbit since 1999; it photographs X-rays from hot gas and other
  violent places. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **chaos**: behaviour that never settles and where tiny changes lead to completely different results. →
  [Bifurcation Diagram: in plain English](sketches/bifurcationDiagram.md)
- **clamp**: keep a number between a lowest and highest value. → [GLSL basics: reading a
  shader](glsl-basics.md)
- **Clifford torus**: the perfectly balanced doughnut in the Hopf fibration, where every pair of its circles
  links once. → [Hopf Fibration: in plain English](sketches/hopfFibration.md)
- **clustering coefficient**: how often two of someone's friends are also friends with each other, averaged
  over a network. → [Six Degrees of Poop: in plain English](sketches/smallWorld.md)
- **code notes**: the hand-written explanations, line by line, that make a sketch's code and equation
  clickable. → [Clickable code and symbols: how it works](clickable-code.md)
- **colour scale**: a rule turning a measured number into a colour you can read back. → [Colour 4: colour
  scales for data, and how they are chosen](colour-scales.md)
- **colour-matching functions**: three curves (CIE 1931) saying how strongly each wavelength of light looks
  red, green and blue to a typical eye; used to turn a spectrum into a colour. → [Soap Film Colours: in plain
  English](sketches/soapFilmColours.md)
- **comedy formula**: a true equation × an absurd noun, told deadpan (`src/lib/bit.js`).
- **comment**: a note for people, after `//` or between `/*` and `*/`, ignored by the computer. → [Reading
  JavaScript 1: values, names and comments](js-values-and-variables.md)
- **complex number**: two numbers bundled together, like a position across and up, that can be multiplied. →
  [Audio-Reactive Julia Set: in plain English](sketches/audioReactiveJulia.md) (math page *(planned)*)
- **compression**: storing information in fewer bits by squeezing out repetition and predictability. → [She
  Sells Sea Shells, Compressed: in plain English](sketches/huffmanSeaShells.md)
- **connectome**: a wiring diagram of a brain: every neuron and who it talks to. → [Fly Connectome: in plain
  English](sketches/flyConnectome.md)
- **const / let**: make a named value; `const` never changes, `let` can. → [Reading JavaScript 1: values,
  names and comments](js-values-and-variables.md)
- **constraint propagation**: after making one decision, crossing off options elsewhere that no longer fit,
  and following on from those. → [Wave Function Collapse: in plain English](sketches/waveFunctionCollapse.md)
- **control point**: a point that pulls a curve towards it. A Bézier curve starts on the first control point
  and ends on the last, but usually misses the ones in between. → [De Casteljau Bézier: in plain
  English](sketches/bezierDeCasteljau.md)
- **Coriolis effect**: in a turning frame (like the spinning Earth), the apparent sideways push on anything
  moving. → [Lagrange Point Parking: in plain English](sketches/lagrangeParking.md)
- **cosine palette**: the one-line rainbow recipe: red, green and blue each rise and fall, out of step. →
  [Colour 3: the cosine palette (the rainbow trick)](cosine-palette.md)
- **cosmic microwave background**: the faint glow left over from the Big Bang, filling the whole sky; moving
  through it is the universe's own way of measuring "standing still". → [Galaxy motions and the shapes they
  leave](galaxy-motions.md)
- **Cosmicflows-4**: a catalogue of about 56,000 galaxy distances, and the map of matter and motion worked out
  from it (Courtois et al. 2023). → [Galaxy motions and the shapes they leave](galaxy-motions.md)
- **crystal**: a solid whose atoms or molecules are arranged in a regular repeating pattern. → [Snowflake
  Growth: in plain English](sketches/snowflakeGrowth.md)
- **ctx**: the bag of useful things the app hands a sketch: camera, scene, sliders, time. → [The frame loop:
  setup, update, dispose](frame-loop.md)
- **damping**: losing a little speed every step, like moving through honey, so things settle. →
  [Force-Directed Graph: in plain English](sketches/forceDirectedGraph.md)
- **DataTexture**: a grid of numbers made in code and handed to a shader as a picture. → [Textures: pictures a
  shader can look things up in](textures.md)
- **delta (`ctx.delta`)**: seconds since the last frame, about 0.017. → [The frame loop: setup, update,
  dispose](frame-loop.md)
- **density**: how much mass is packed into each unit of volume (water: 1,000 kg per cubic metre). → [Roche
  Limit Shredder: in plain English](sketches/rocheShredder.md)
- **destructuring**: taking several named values out of an object in one line, `const { a, b } = …`. →
  [Reading JavaScript 4: lists (arrays) and labelled boxes (objects)](js-arrays-and-objects.md)
- **diffusion**: spreading out from where there is more to where there is less, like ink in water. →
  [Belousov–Zhabotinsky: in plain English](sketches/belousovZhabotinsky.md)
- **Dijkstra's algorithm**: a shortest-route search that always explores the nearest unexplored place next,
  spreading out in rings. → [Pathfinding Race: in plain English](sketches/pathfindingRace.md)
- **dispersion**: light of different colours bending by different amounts, because the refractive index
  depends on wavelength; it is what makes a prism's rainbow. → [Prism Dispersion: in plain
  English](sketches/prismDispersion.md)
- **dispose**: the clean-up step when you leave a sketch. → [The frame loop: setup, update,
  dispose](frame-loop.md)
- **diverging scale**: a colour scale with two colours meeting at a pale middle, for "above or below average".
  → [Colour 4: colour scales for data, and how they are chosen](colour-scales.md)
- **domain warping**: looking up noise at a position that has itself been pushed by noise; makes fake fluids.
  → [Fluid Dye: in plain English](sketches/fluidDye.md)
- **Doppler effect**: a moving source looks bluer and brighter coming towards you and redder going away, like
  a passing siren's pitch. → [Black Hole Lensing: in plain English](sketches/blackHoleLensing.md)
- **dot product**: multiply two arrows' matching parts and add: big and positive when they point the same way,
  negative when opposite. → [Hawks and Boids: in plain English](sketches/hawksAndBoids.md)
- **eccentricity**: how stretched an oval orbit is: 0 is a circle, near 1 a very long thin oval. → [Kepler's
  Pizza: in plain English](sketches/keplersPizza.md)
- **ecological incumbency**: whoever already holds a way of life keeps it, because newcomers must out-compete an
  animal already good at the job. → [The Empty Throne: in plain English](sketches/emptyThrone.md)
- **edge (graph)**: a link between two dots in a network. → [Force-Directed Graph: in plain
  English](sketches/forceDirectedGraph.md)
- **ellipse**: an oval: the shape of every planet's orbit, with the star at one of two special points inside
  it (a focus), not the centre. → [Kepler's Pizza: in plain English](sketches/keplersPizza.md)
- **emergence**: order arising from simple local rules, with no leader. → [Boids Flock: in plain
  English](sketches/boidsFlock.md)
- **entropy**: a measure of disorder, or of how much information is missing about the details; the second law
  says it never decreases overall. → [Maxwell's Demon: in plain English](sketches/maxwellsDemon.md)
- **epicycle**: a circle riding on the rim of another circle; Ptolemy used them for planets, Fourier showed
  any closed drawing is made of them. → [Fourier Epicycles: in plain English](sketches/fourierEpicycles.md)
- **escape time**: how many rounds of a rule a point survives before flying off; it colours most fractals. →
  [Audio-Reactive Julia Set: in plain English](sketches/audioReactiveJulia.md)
- **Euler's method**: the simplest way to step a simulation forward: new value = old value + speed × time
  step. → [Kuramoto–Sivashinsky: in plain English](sketches/kuramotoSivashinsky.md)
- **event file**: an X-ray telescope's raw data: a list of every single X-ray particle that hit the camera, with
  where, when and how energetic. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **event horizon**: the point of no return round a black hole; nothing that crosses it comes back out. →
  [Black Hole Lensing: in plain English](sketches/blackHoleLensing.md)
- **excitable**: quiet until sparked, then it fires a wave and needs time to recover. → [Belousov–Zhabotinsky:
  in plain English](sketches/belousovZhabotinsky.md)
- **exoplanet**: a planet orbiting a star other than the Sun. The first were confirmed in 1992; over 6,000 are
  known now. → [Six Thousand Worlds: in plain English](sketches/exoplanets.md)
- **exposure map**: how long each spot of the sky was really watched in an observation, so gaps between camera
  chips don't look like dark sky. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **extra dimensions**: directions of space beyond the three we know; physicists study what gravity would do
  with more of them. → [Dark Saturn: in plain English](sketches/darkSaturn.md)
- **fbm**: noise stacked in layers, each twice as detailed and half as strong. → [Noise: randomness that looks
  natural](noise.md)
- **fetch**: ask for a file over the network. → [Reading JavaScript 5: loading data (`fetch`, `Promise`,
  `await`)](js-promises-and-fetch.md)
- **field**: one labelled box in a sketch file, like `name` or `update`. → [The sketch format: every
  field](sketch-format.md)
- **fitness**: in biology, how many surviving young a kind of body leaves; not how athletic it is. → [Adaptive
  landscapes](adaptive-landscape.md)
- **flat shading**: lighting each triangle as one flat face, for a cut-gem look. → [Displaced Icosphere: in
  plain English](sketches/displacedIcosphere.md)
- **float**: in GLSL, one number with a decimal point; write `1.0`, not `1`. → [GLSL basics: reading a
  shader](glsl-basics.md)
- **force-directed layout**: drawing a network by letting dots repel and links pull like springs. →
  [Force-Directed Graph: in plain English](sketches/forceDirectedGraph.md)
- **Foucault pendulum**: a long pendulum whose swing slowly turns as the Earth turns beneath it, once a day at
  the poles; first shown in Paris in 1851. → [Foucault's Pendulum: in plain
  English](sketches/foucaultPendulum.md)
- **Fourier coefficient**: the size and starting angle of one circle (one wave) in a Fourier sum. → [Fourier
  Epicycles: in plain English](sketches/fourierEpicycles.md)
- **Fourier transform**: splitting a pattern into a mix of plain waves, like an equaliser splits music into
  bass and treble. → [Kuramoto–Sivashinsky: the bug that froze the flame](sketches/kuramotoSivashinsky-fix.md)
- **fractal**: a shape with detail at every zoom level, often made by repeating one simple step many times. →
  [Life-Warped Fractal: in plain English](sketches/lifeWarpedFractal.md)
- **fractional residual**: how much brighter or dimmer each spot is than its smooth surroundings (0.05 = 5%
  brighter); it makes faint ripples visible. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **fragment shader**: the shader that picks the colour of each pixel. → [Shaders: two questions](shaders.md)
- **frame**: one picture of the animation; about 60 are drawn each second. → [The frame loop: setup, update,
  dispose](frame-loop.md)
- **frame dragging**: a spinning mass dragging space round with it, so nearby things are turned too. → [Dark
  Saturn: in plain English](sketches/darkSaturn.md)
- **frequency**: how many waves (or swings, or turns) happen per second, measured in hertz (Hz). → [Sonic
  Boom: in plain English](sketches/sonicBoom.md)
- **Fresnel effect**: surfaces seen edge-on reflect more than surfaces seen face-on, like a lake at a low
  angle. → [Iridescent Sphere: in plain English](sketches/iridescentSphere.md)
- **function**: a named, reusable recipe with inputs and a result. → [Reading JavaScript 2: functions and
  arrows (`=>`)](js-functions.md)
- **gain**: multiplying brightness up, like a microphone's gain knob. → [Colour 2: blending, adding, dimming
  and gain](colour-blending-and-gain.md)
- **galaxy cluster**: hundreds to thousands of galaxies held together by gravity, all bathed in one huge cloud
  of gas at tens of millions of degrees. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **Gaussian curvature**: how strongly a surface curves at a spot, in a way bending without stretching can't
  change. → [Helicoid ↔ Catenoid: in plain English](sketches/helicoidCatenoid.md)
- **gene**: in a genetic algorithm, one number of a candidate's recipe (a size, a timing, a grip) that its
  children inherit, slightly changed. → [Silly Walks, Evolved: in plain
  English](sketches/sillyWalksEvolved.md)
- **general relativity**: Einstein's theory of gravity, in which mass bends space and time, and so bends
  light. → [Black Hole Lensing: in plain English](sketches/blackHoleLensing.md)
- **genetic algorithm**: evolution by natural selection on a computer: test many candidates, keep the best,
  copy them with small random changes, repeat. → [Silly Walks, Evolved: in plain
  English](sketches/sillyWalksEvolved.md)
- **genetic drift**: random change in a species from the luck of who happens to breed. → [Adaptive
  landscapes](adaptive-landscape.md)
- **geometry**: an object's shape, as corners and triangles. → [Drawing in 3D 2: mesh, geometry and
  material](mesh-geometry-material.md)
- **glider**: a five-square pattern in the Game of Life that crawls diagonally forever. → [Conway's Game of
  Life: in plain English](sketches/gameOfLife.md)
- **glow**: blurring the brightest parts of the picture and adding them back, so bright things shine. → [Glow:
  making bright things shine](glow.md)
- **GLSL**: the language shaders are written in. → [GLSL basics: reading a shader](glsl-basics.md)
- **golden angle**: about 137.5°, the turn that spaces sunflower seeds; used so loops never repeat exactly. →
  [Belousov–Zhabotinsky: in plain English](sketches/belousovZhabotinsky.md)
- **graph (network)**: dots joined by links, like a metro map. → [Force-Directed Graph: in plain
  English](sketches/forceDirectedGraph.md)
- **gravitational wave**: a ripple in space itself, stretching and squeezing it, sent out by heavy objects
  such as merging black holes. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **growth factor**: one number saying how far gravity has pulled the universe's lumps along since the
  beginning: 0 at the start, 1 today. → [Galaxy motions and the shapes they leave](galaxy-motions.md)
- **habitable zone**: the band round a star where a planet like Earth could keep liquid water on its surface.
  → [Six Thousand Worlds: in plain English](sketches/exoplanets.md)
- **hash**: a scrambler that turns a position into a random-looking but repeatable number. → [Noise:
  randomness that looks natural](noise.md)
- **helicoid**: the spiral-staircase soap film you'd get on a twisted wire. → [Helicoid ↔ Catenoid: in plain
  English](sketches/helicoidCatenoid.md)
- **helioseismology**: studying the inside of the Sun from the sound waves ringing through it, seen as its
  surface moving up and down. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **heuristic**: a rule of thumb or educated guess that speeds up a search. → [Pathfinding Race: in plain
  English](sketches/pathfindingRace.md)
- **hexagonal grid**: a grid of six-sided cells, each with six neighbours at equal distances, like a
  honeycomb. → [Snowflake Growth: in plain English](sketches/snowflakeGrowth.md)
- **histogram**: a bar chart counting how often each result happened. → [Galton Board: in plain
  English](sketches/galtonBoard.md)
- **Hopf fibration**: the "3-sphere" (the three-dimensional surface of a ball in four dimensions) split into
  circles, every pair of them linked like chain links. → [Hopf Fibration: in plain
  English](sketches/hopfFibration.md)
- **HSL**: a colour as hue (place on the colour wheel), saturation (vividness) and lightness. → [Colour 1:
  RGB, three numbers](rgb.md)
- **Huffman coding**: a compression code that gives common symbols short codes and rare ones long codes, built
  by repeatedly joining the two rarest. → [She Sells Sea Shells, Compressed: in plain
  English](sketches/huffmanSeaShells.md)
- **Huygens' principle**: every point a wave reaches acts as a new source of little ripples, and the wave
  ahead is their sum. → [Double Slit, With Eraser: in plain English](sketches/doubleSlitEraser.md)
- **hyperbolic geometry**: a world with more room the further out you go, like a crinkly lettuce leaf. →
  [Hyperbolic Tiling: in plain English](sketches/hyperbolicTiling.md)
- **if / else, `? :`**: choose what to do depending on a question. → [Reading JavaScript 3: decisions (`if`)
  and loops (`for`)](js-decisions-and-loops.md)
- **infinity (point at)**: in the complex plane drawn on a sphere, the single extra point where everything far
  away meets; it is the top of the sphere. → [Stereographic Projection: in plain
  English](sketches/stereographicMobius.md)
- **information cascade**: people copying what those around them do instead of checking for themselves, so one
  belief sweeps a crowd. → [Hawks and Boids: in plain English](sketches/hawksAndBoids.md)
- **instancing**: drawing many copies of one shape in a single go. → [Drawing in 3D 6: many copies of one
  shape (instancing)](instancing.md)
- **integral**: in calculus, a running total; the integral of a wave is another, shifted wave. → [Radian628
  Integral-Warped GLSL Fractal: in plain English](sketches/different-this-time.md) (math page *(planned)*)
- **interference**: waves adding up where they agree and cancelling where they don't. → [Caustics: in plain
  English](sketches/causticsLight.md)
- **iridescence**: rainbow colours that change with the angle you look from, as on a soap bubble. →
  [Iridescent Sphere: in plain English](sketches/iridescentSphere.md)
- **isometry**: a bend that changes a shape without stretching or squashing any part of it. → [Helicoid ↔
  Catenoid: in plain English](sketches/helicoidCatenoid.md)
- **JSON**: a text format of labelled values, used for `meta.json` files. → [Reading JavaScript 5: loading
  data (`fetch`, `Promise`, `await`)](js-promises-and-fetch.md)
- **Julia set**: under the rule "square it, add c", the points that never fly off fill a shape (the *filled*
  Julia set); the Julia set is that shape's edge, where the fine lace is. → [Audio-Reactive Julia Set: in
  plain English](sketches/audioReactiveJulia.md)
- **kaleidoscope fold**: cutting the picture into wedges and folding them all onto one. → [Radian628 — Abyssal
  Bloom: in plain English](sketches/chatgpt-quantum.md)
- **kelvin**: the temperature scale that starts at absolute zero: 0 K = −273.15 °C, and 293 K is 20 °C. →
  [Mirage: in plain English](sketches/mirage.md)
- **Kepler's laws**: three rules for orbits found by Johannes Kepler (1609, 1619): orbits are ellipses; equal
  areas are swept in equal times; the time per orbit squared equals the size cubed (T² = a³, in years and AU).
  → [Kepler's Pizza: in plain English](sketches/keplersPizza.md)
- **kiloparsec**: (kpc) a thousand parsecs, about 3,260 light-years; a handy ruler for the insides of galaxies
  and clusters. → [Sounds of Space: in plain English](sketches/soundsOfSpace.md)
- **Klein bottle**: a bottle whose inside is its outside; in three dimensions it must pass through itself. →
  [Klein Bottle: in plain English](sketches/kleinBottle.md)
- **Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation**: the classic equation for a wrinkling flame front that never settles. →
  [Kuramoto–Sivashinsky: in plain English](sketches/kuramotoSivashinsky.md)
- **L-system**: a rewriting rule (replace every letter by a phrase, again and again) whose result is read as
  drawing instructions; invented to model plant growth. → [L-System Botanical Garden: in plain
